# smart_forum
Smart-Forum eliminates exploitative middlemen pricing, reduces post-harvest wastage by evaluating seasonal/surplus produce before purchase, and ensures farmers get fair, data-backed prices while customers get transparent, reliable supply.
Smart-Forum analyses the data from genuine sources, and calculates the potentials of the products and suggests an effective and efficient way to complete the work, and simulating a potential profit margin, and suggesting potential ways to prevent wastage of products.
